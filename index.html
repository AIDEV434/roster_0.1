Built for Teams

Set up your company profile once. Stay protected forever.

ROSTER is built around the concept of a Trust Roster -- a profile of your organisation. You add your company domains, your executives, your finance team, and any trusted external contacts.

Once your roster is set, every suspicious email gets checked against it. If someone claims to be your CEO but is sending from a different domain, ROSTER catches it instantly. If a new domain is registered that looks just like yours, ROSTER flags it.

Perfect for agencies, SMBs, law firms, finance teams -- any organisation where a single impersonation email can cause serious damage.
